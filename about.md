# About

I am a quantitative researcher with an interest in technology. I started this blog to write about things that I like, share my thoughts, and record my own learning journey.

I studied statistics and mathematics in college, and was trained as a financial economist in graduate school, with a focus on empirical asset pricing, econometrics, and machine learning.

Anything I write here reflect my views, and my views only.
